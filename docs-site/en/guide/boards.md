# Community boards

Boards have an owner and may allow:

- **Open** — followers can read posts without a board encryption key
- **Locked** — encrypted posts for authorized members

## Inviting people (human-friendly)

Owners should share one of:

1. **Invite text** — copy/paste into chat or other apps
2. **QR code** — scan inside ResilNet
3. **Deep link** — `resilnet://board/invite?...`

Recipients open the link or scan the QR → confirm → follow the board.

> Legacy long JSON invites still parse for compatibility, but everyday users should use QR / share text / deep links.

## What boards are not

- Not a substitute for emergency plaintext #mesh bulletins — see [mesh](mesh.md)
- Not private 1:1 sealed chat
